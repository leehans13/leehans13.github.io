Research

Design-aware statistical inference

Under review Bioinformatics · Oxford University Press

Condition assignment determines the appropriate differential gene expression test in single-cell RNA sequencing studies

Hanshin Lee, Hanbin Lee, Buhm Han, and Minwoo Kim

In multi-subject single-cell studies, comparison labels may be assigned at the subject level or the cell level. Treating these designs as interchangeable can identify the wrong sampling unit and misstate the uncertainty of a differential-expression test.

  1. 01 Study design How labels are assigned
  2. 02 Sampling unit Subject or cell
  3. 03 Variance scale O(1/n) or O(1/N)
  4. 04 Error control Type I and type II errors

Methods

M-estimation, Taylor expansion, central limit theory, and subject- and cell-level variance decomposition.

Contribution

Establishes how the valid sampling unit changes across two label-assignment scenarios and why variance misspecification can inflate testing errors.

Open research

Reproducible simulation and perturbation-data analyses using public multi-subject single-cell datasets.
