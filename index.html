Welcome!

M.S. in Data Science · Pusan National University

B.S. in Physics, Minor in Economics · Pusan National University

I am a graduate researcher interested in statistical methodology, machine learning, and quantitative applications in economics and finance.

I currently work with Prof. Minwoo Kim in Statistics. My research examines how study design and dependence structures determine the appropriate unit of inference. In my current project, I use M-estimation and variance decomposition to study differential-expression testing in multi-subject single-cell data.

My academic path from physics to data science has shaped my interest in mathematical modeling and reliable inference. I plan to apply to Statistics PhD programs for Fall 2027.

Research interests

  • Statistical machine learning
  • Inference for dependent and high-dimensional data
  • Quantitative applications in economics and finance
